The Trading Floor

250 neural bots trade real players' cards (every design, finish, kit, Prizm parallel and autograph) at the site's real prices, replaying the save's real seasons. Each bot is two small networks with random weights. Nobody copies anybody: a bot's networks pass to its heir when it dies, and newcomers arrive with fresh ones.
