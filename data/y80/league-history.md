## Eras

### 1. Real-roster era (Y1–15)
Imported NFL rosters set the early marks. Lamar Jackson won 2 MVPs in the save on top of his 2 real ones. Drake Maye (3 MVPs, 1 ring) likely won your first title in Y3. Mason Taylor's 3 rings match the Dolphins' 3 titles; Akiem Bouye and Taywan Tuttle's 4 match the Chargers' 4. T. Watt holds the career sack record and Z. Griffen the 54-yd field goal (Y7).

### 2. Commanders run (Y16–19)
Four straight titles. Alexx Sanchez's 4 rings fit as the QB, but Jayden Daniels and Avonte Gooden are also listed as Commanders, so this is unconfirmed. Ian Finney's career starts here.

### 3. Waller–Finney era (Y20–26)
Ray Waller ("Ray Comeback Waller") won 5 rings and 5 MVPs with the Bucs, matching their 5 titles. Ian Finney ("The Infinity Man") had Tyrion Kline ("FBI") as his WR2. Finney's 9 rings = 4 Commanders + 5 Bucs. Waller's 513-yd game (Y26) and Finney's 6-TD game (Y23) date here.

**The rival:** Kyric McQuay ("Baby Manning"), Colts. Beat you in LXXXI (38–32) and LXXXIII (29–28), SB MVP both times. 43 INTs in 10,099 attempts, the best ball security in the league. He's the only QB who beat you consistently without playing for you. His raw talent score (968) beats Waller's (900).

### 4. Bills dynasty (Y27–33)
Dennis Baylon ("The Iron Island") went 7-for-7 in Super Bowls. Charlie John, Tyrone Baugh and Rob Lowe also have exactly 7 rings. Reuben Kilian ("Mr. Consistent") was the WR1. Baylon's 7,452-yd season (Y33) is the passing season record, and his 3.55 TPG is the best of any QB. Cairo Asanjh ("The Prophecy") chased Finney's yardage record here; his 9 rings = 7 Bills + 2 Seahawks.

Outside your team: the Falcons won XCIV (Y35), likely behind Derrick Myrick (19,801 yds, 347 TD, 248 fumbles).

### 5. Ikarras era (Y36–46)
Rondale Ikarras ("The Prodigal Son") won 9 rings, matching your 9 titles across the Seahawks, Chargers and Ravens. He set the 32-TD season (Y38), 5-TD game (Y40), 2,104-yd season (Y42, 1 yard short of Dickerson's real record) and career rushing marks. 4 OPOYs, 5 SB MVPs, **0 MVPs**. Asanjh's final season came alongside him on the Seahawks. Wes Sione (0.78 TD/game, best of any TE) and OL Jawill Perkins played here.

### 6. Brandson era (Y43–62)
Sherman Brandson ("General Sherman"): 91,463 yds, 1,150 TD, 85% completions, 8 MVPs, 10 rings, 0 career fumbles. Set the 9-TD game and 94-TD season (Y55). Only 4 SB MVPs in 10 rings, plus playoff losses in CIX (Y50, with Denzel Hayes at RB) and CXIX (Y60). Retired injured mid-season.

### 7. Vikings dynasty (Y59–69)
God Johnson (formerly Mason Earl III) broke Finney's career receiving mark: 36,005 yds, 378 TD, 10 rings, 3 fumbles, 4 of 6 receiving records. Jackson W. Kline, Tyrion's son, played beside him with 6 OPOYs, the most in the league. Pharoah Hunter set the 288-yd game (Y68). The Wilson brothers likely played QB: Wayne (431 games, 90,450 yds, 5 MVPs) and Michael (58,992 yds, 6 MVPs, retired injured mid-season). Combined 11 MVPs and 10 rings.

### 8. Modern era (Y70–80)
Titans three-peat (Y70–72), then a Super Bowl loss to those Titans as the Seahawks (Y74), and the Jets title (Y77). Malakai Sawyer set the 212-yd game (Y78) and 65-yd run (Y75, from Jason Macleone) but has no Super Bowl appearances in those seasons. Season Y80 ends in Super Bowl CXXXIX.

## Your spreadsheet metrics

- **How they work:** Dominance = 0.7 × True Talent + award points (ring 60, MVP 50, OPOY 30, SB MVP 40, DPOY 120, ROTY 0). Career Legacy ≈ 4.6 × Talent + 3.9 × Dominance. TPG ≈ talent per game.
- **Main flaw:** a ring is worth more than an MVP, and every ring went to your team, so the metric rewards being on your roster.
- **Overrated:** Waller (21st in talent, 10th in legacy); dynasty role players Rob Lowe, Deante Elder, Damone Omameh, Taywan Tuttle; Brady (real-life awards); Wayne Wilson (lowest TPG of the elite QBs).
- **Underrated:** Bryant (5th in talent, 13th in legacy); Cooper Dean; Mathieu (17th → 32nd); McQuay; Ikarras (sheet shows 0 OPOY instead of 4, about 470 legacy points short); Baylon (best QB TPG).
- **TPG favors short careers:** Sione 4.95, Perkins 4.79, Benkert 3.27, Jewell 3.41.
- **Suggested fixes:** MVP above ring (e.g. 80 vs 40); DPOY near MVP level; score real-life legends separately; a ~150-game minimum for TPG.
